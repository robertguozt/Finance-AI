## Key findings
- The `recommend` stage accounts for 87% of total latency — the sole bottleneck.
- It serially fetches 30 tickers one by one, causing 30 of the 31 yfinance calls.
- Optimization target: parallelize / replace live fetches with a local historical table to eliminate the 25s.
